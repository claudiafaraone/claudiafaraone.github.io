Claudia Faraone is an architect and urbanist. She is a researcher in Urban Planning and Design at the [Department of Architecture](https://www.dda.unich.it/) of Università G. d'Annunzio Chieti-Pescara, where she teaches urban planning and climate change adaptation.

She graduated in Architecture at Università Iuav di Venezia (2004), specialised in the joint programme EMU – European Postgraduate Master in Urbanism at UPC Barcelona, TU Delft and KU Leuven (2007), and completed a European PhD at the Urban Studies Department of Roma Tre University and LAA – Laboratoire Architecture Anthropologie, ENSA Paris-La Villette (2011).

Before moving to Chieti-Pescara she was research fellow at Università degli Studi di Udine (2022–2023), within the coordination of the regional spatial plan of Friuli Venezia Giulia, and at Università Iuav di Venezia, where since 2011 she has been conducting research and teaching as a member of the action-research groups on urban regeneration and urban design *Living Urban Scape*, *Re-Cycle Italy*, *MargheraLab*, *Interreg DIVA* and *CityLab*. She has been visiting research fellow at Université Libre de Bruxelles – La Cambre (LOUISE, 2018), Ghent University and the University of East London, and adjunct professor in urban design at Venice International University.

Her research deals with the cultures of (re)production of urban space, with its temporalities and practices: from large urban landscape transformations after natural disasters and socio-economic disruptions, to neighbourhood regeneration, the productive city and the relationship between work and urban space. Since 2004 she has been producing publications, workshops, exhibitions and audio-visual ethnographies, exhibited among others at the Venice Architecture Biennale (2014–2018), the City Museum of La Spezia (2018) and in Comacchio (2013).

She is founding member and vice-president of the association for urban research [ETICity – Exploring Territories, Imagining the City](https://www.eticity.it) (2012), and since 2017 she coordinates its Venice unit, [ETICity Venezia](/eticity-venezia/).
