## Research themes

- **Cultures of (re)production of urban space** — the temporalities and everyday practices through which cities are made and remade.
- **Urban regeneration and the productive city** — work, manufacturing and urban policies in post-industrial territories, from Porto Marghera and Mestre to Brussels.
- **Landscapes of disaster and transition** — large urban transformations after natural disasters and socio-economic disruptions, from Skopje to L'Aquila.
- **Public space and in-between spaces** — informal uses, play and appropriation of urban space.
- **Climate change adaptation** — urban planning and design for adaptation.
- **Visual and audio-visual ethnography** — video as a tool for urban research. See [Audiovisual](/audiovisual/).
