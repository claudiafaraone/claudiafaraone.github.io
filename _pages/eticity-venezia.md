[ETICity – Exploring Territories, Imagining the City](https://www.eticity.it) is an association for urban research founded in 2012 by a group of women researchers in urban studies, to build projects in which research serves cities and their territories. Through urban exploration, mapping, training, action-research and local development projects, it looks at the city as a place of encounter, coexistence, experimentation and collective action. Claudia Faraone is a founding member and vice-president of the association.

**ETICity Venezia**, the Venice local unit, was established in 2017 and has been coordinated by Claudia Faraone since then. It works in Mestre, and in particular in the Piave neighbourhood between the railway station and the city centre, on local development projects and initiatives together with associations, residents and informal groups. It also offers training and urban-knowledge itineraries, and contributes to conferences, workshops and seminars.

## Projects

### CORALE – Veneto network of hybrid socio-cultural spaces (2022–ongoing)

![CORALE](/images/eticity/corale.jpg){: .project-img}

In June 2022, during the RoadShow of *Lo Stato dei Luoghi*, ETICity, together with Elena Ostanel (Università Iuav di Venezia) and Lo Stato dei Luoghi, launched an informal network of the social and cultural spaces of the Veneto region born from bottom-up regeneration processes. The network supports the exchange of practices among these spaces and opens a dialogue with institutions, public administrations and foundations to co-design support policies.

[Network dossier (PDF)](https://www.eticity.it/w/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Dossier_Corale_agg_3agosto.pdf)

### Celestini Social District – Centro Sportivo Montessori, Chirignago (2022–2023)

![Celestini Social District](/images/eticity/celestini.jpg){: .project-img .project-img--small}

The Centro Sportivo Montessori is a municipally owned space in Chirignago, born from the initiative of a group of families who, in 2011, imagined turning a large abandoned area into a meeting place for all ages. With the association I Celestini, ETICity promoted a civic crowdfunding campaign within the programme of the City of Venice to improve the centre's spaces and activities, following the "Social District" model, and curated a co-design workshop with the UNESCO Chair of Università Iuav di Venezia (March 2023).

[Crowdfunding campaign](https://www.produzionidalbasso.com/project/celestini-social-district) · [Iuav UNESCO Chair workshop](http://www.unescochair-iuav.it/blog/chirignagosocialdistrict/)

### Portineria di Quartiere Mestre – Via Piave (2021–ongoing)

A neighbourhood concierge at QPi, the concierge and shared courtyard at Via Piave 65 in Mestre: a place for listening, support and first assistance for residents, offering small local services, promoting volunteering and organising social activities. It is part of *Portinerie di Quartiere – Avamposto di Comunità*, a project of CAVV – CSV di Venezia with Leroy Merlin, led by ADA con Venezia with ETICity and other local associations.

[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/portineria_corte_mestre)

### Bottegattiva – Vivere il Quartiere Piave (2021)

A project promoted by CNA Metropolitana di Venezia and funded by the Venice Rovigo Chamber of Commerce, in partnership with Università Iuav di Venezia. ETICity mapped about one hundred craft and retail businesses in the Piave neighbourhood, managed relations with the neighbourhood and organised the project events; twenty businesses joined the project and were included in a map of the neighbourhood.

### MMA – Mestre Mon Amour (2018–2019)

![Mestre Mon Amour](/images/eticity/mestre-mon-amour.jpg){: .project-img .project-img--portrait}

A festival of contemporary dance and performing arts for urban regeneration, created with the association Vene within the #tuttamialacittà programme of CSV Venezia. Its first edition (September 2019) brought performances, masterclasses, video-dance screenings and talks into the streets and squares of central Mestre and the Piave neighbourhood, to turn marginal areas into places of cultural exchange and sociability.

[Website](https://www.mestremonamour.it/) · [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mestremonamour/)

### Riverberi – Il vuoto s'illumina (2017–2018)

![Riverberi](/images/eticity/riverberi.jpg){: .project-img}

An urban regeneration proposal for Via Piave and its neighbourhood, promoted by ETICity Venezia with Gruppo di Lavoro via Piave. Its first phase, *Il vuoto s'illumina* ("the void lights up"), reactivated vacant shops along Via Piave with cultural production – art projects, start-ups, innovative crafts and technology – hosting exhibitions and workshops from March 2018.

[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/riverberi/) · [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/progettoriverberi/)

### Urban Rise (2017–ongoing)

![Urban Rise](/images/eticity/urban-rise.jpg){: .project-img}

Since December 2017 ETICity Venezia has been part of the Urban Rise collective, which coordinates urban art and active citizenship initiatives for the regeneration of the Venetian mainland. Projects include the exhibition *Vista Muro* on street art as a tool for urban regeneration (January 2018), the contest for a mural on the façade of the Cinema Dante d'essai, and the street art festival *Battle of Dante* (May 2018).

[Urban Rise](https://www.urbanrise.it/)

### Lucid Schizophrenia – Monditalia, 14th Venice Architecture Biennale (2014)

![Lucid Schizophrenia at the Venice Biennale](/images/eticity/lucid-schizophrenia.jpg){: .project-img}

Before the Venice unit was established, ETICity curated *Lucid Schizophrenia* in the Weekend Specials of Monditalia at the 14th International Architecture Exhibition: a workshop with fifty students from Iuav, Roma Tre and L'Aquila universities (Corderie dell'Arsenale, 16–21 September 2014) on the management of public heritage in Italy, between rhetoric and practice — from abandoned small towns to post-earthquake L'Aquila and public real estate in Rome.
